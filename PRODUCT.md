# Military Strategy Database

## Product truth

Military Strategy Database is an educational reference tool for comparing
campaign patterns, strategic objectives, and operational constraints. It is a
curated study interface, not a live intelligence source and not advice for
real-world violence.

## First release

- Browse a small, clearly labelled reference dataset.
- Filter by era and strategic theme.
- Open a campaign record and compare its objective, constraint, and lesson.
- Keep every claim scoped to the supplied study notes.

## Evidence boundary

The first release is a public reference prototype with self-authored sample
records. It does not claim comprehensive historical coverage or expert review.
